# Day 10 Learnings – Meta Ads

## Campaign
A campaign is the highest level in Meta Ads. It defines the marketing objective, such as sales, leads, or website traffic.

## Ad Set
An ad set controls who sees the ads. It includes audience, budget, schedule, and placements.

## Ad
An ad is the actual creative shown to users, including images, videos, headlines, text, and CTA.

---

## Key Metrics

### CPM (Cost Per Mille)
The cost of 1,000 ad impressions.
Lower CPM usually means cheaper reach.

### CTR (Click Through Rate)
Percentage of users who clicked the ad after seeing it.

CTR = (Clicks / Impressions) × 100

Higher CTR indicates engaging ads.

### ROAS (Return On Ad Spend)
Revenue earned for every ₹1 spent on ads.

ROAS = Revenue / Ad Spend

Higher ROAS means better profitability.

### CAC (Customer Acquisition Cost)
Average cost to acquire one customer.

CAC = Total Ad Spend / Number of Customers

Lower CAC is better.

---

## Media Plan

A media plan is a strategy that defines:
- Target audience
- Budget
- Platforms
- Ad schedule
- Ad formats
- Campaign goals
- Performance metrics

It helps businesses spend advertising money efficiently.
