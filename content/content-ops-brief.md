Idea → Brief → Content Creation → Internal Review with the team → Client Review/Approval → changes added → Scheduling → Published

##  Review & Approval

Review and Approval
Content is checked for quality and accuracy.
Content is reviewed to ensure it follows the client’s brand guidelines.
Captions, visuals, spelling, and formatting are checked.
Any required changes or corrections are shared with the content creator.
The creator makes the required revisions.
The final content is sent to the client for approval.
Once approved, the content is scheduled for publishing.

##  New Joiner Quick Guide

When I receive a new content task:

1. Check the brief.
2. Identify the client and required platform.
3. Check the client's brand guidelines.
4. Find the required assets/templates.
5. Create the content according to the brief.
6. Submit it through [ticketing agent].
7. Wait for review/feedback from [client].
8. Make revisions if required.
9. Once approved, follow the publishing/scheduling process.
