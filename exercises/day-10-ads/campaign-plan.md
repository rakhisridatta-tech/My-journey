# Meta Ads Campaign Plan

## Product
GlowBox – Personalized Gift Box

## Objective
Increase online sales and generate website purchases through Meta (Facebook & Instagram) ads.

## Target Audience
- Age: 18–35 years
- Gender: All
- Location: India
- Interests:
  - Gift shopping
  - Birthdays
  - Personalized gifts
  - Online shopping
  - Instagram users

## Budget
- Daily Budget: ₹1,000
- Campaign Duration: 10 Days
- Total Budget: ₹10,000

## Ad Placement
- Facebook Feed
- Instagram Feed
- Instagram Stories
- Facebook Reels

## Creative Brief

### Headline
Make Every Gift Special!

### Primary Text
Surprise your loved ones with a personalized GlowBox filled with premium gifts. Perfect for birthdays, anniversaries, and special occasions.

### CTA
Shop Now

### Creative
- Bright, colorful gift box images
- Short 15-second video showing unboxing
- Happy customer testimonials

## Success Metrics
- CTR > 3%
- ROAS > 4
- CAC < ₹300
- 100 Purchases
